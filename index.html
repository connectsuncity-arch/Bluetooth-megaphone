💡 Essential DJ Guidelines & Feedback Prevention

01

Beat Matching (Sync)

Enter the base BPM for both loaded tracks. Click **Sync B ➔ A** to match Deck B's tempo to Deck A (it recalculates Deck B's playback rate).

02

Crossfading Curves

The crossfader uses an equal-power trigonometric curve. Sliding it fully left isolates Deck A, and fully right isolates Deck B. Center blends both tracks.

03

MC Voiceover Setup

Adjust the **Voiceover Mic Gain** and **Noise Gate** to enable clean talkover. Turn off "Active" if you want to route direct instrument feed without a gate.

04

Prevent Speaker Squeal

Keep your MC microphone physical position behind the speakers. Use headphones to cue tracks privately to prevent feedback howling.
